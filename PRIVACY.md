# Siren Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**

Siren is an app for listening to audiobooks from your own Audiobookshelf server. It is made by Inceptyon Labs LLC. Siren sends nothing to Inceptyon Labs: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no account with us.

## What Siren stores, and where

- **Your server details.** The server address and your sign-in token are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
- **Downloads and progress.** Books you download, your listening progress, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device. Progress and bookmarks also sync to your Audiobookshelf server, which you control.
- **Settings sync.** If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your preferences sync between your devices through your iCloud account. Inceptyon Labs can't read them.
- **Apple Watch.** Your iPhone shares your server sign-in and playback state with your Apple Watch directly, not through any server of ours.
- **Crash logs.** If Siren crashes, the report is written to the app's log on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

## Who Siren talks to

Only your Audiobookshelf server, at the address you enter, to browse, stream, download and sync. What your server does with that data is up to you and your server's settings.

## Camera, speech and notifications

- **Page Match** uses the camera, or a photo you pick, to read a page of your printed book and find that spot in the audiobook. The text is recognized on your device; no photo is saved or sent.
- **Read Along** and Page Match transcribe the audiobook with Apple's on-device speech models. No audio or text leaves your device. iOS may download a speech model for the book's language.
- **Notifications** are used for alarm alerts only.

## Your choices

You can delete downloads and stored data in Settings, sign out of a server, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

## Children

Siren is for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
